Eiffel Tower Summit 🗼
The Airbnb at 75015 is ~10 min walk to the tower · Plan your visit using the strategy guide below — summit tickets sell out fast, so read this before you go
🗼 Summit Strategy How to Get to the Top
▾
1 Option 1 — Early Morning (Highest Probability)
Arrive at the Eiffel Tower security area 45–60 minutes before opening
Go directly to the ticket booth that sells elevator tickets
Have everyone in your party present
If summit tickets are available, buy them immediately — don't wait to decide on a time
With a 4- and 7-year-old, this is probably the least stressful approach.
2 Option 2 — Evening Backup
If you don't get summit tickets in the morning, return around 8:00–9:00 PM
Crowds often start thinning and additional summit capacity may become available
The view is spectacular — Paris stays light late into the evening in early July, and you'll also see the city transition into nighttime
📲 Keep Checking Online
Start checking the official Eiffel Tower site (toureiffel.paris ↗) several times a day about 1–2 weeks before your trip. Additional inventory and cancellations do sometimes appear.
💛 Family Tip — Evening Visit Is Actually Better
For kids your daughters' ages, the evening visit is worth trying to manage around the schedule. In early July, sunset is around 9:50 PM in Paris, so you can:
1
Have dinner nearby
2
Go up the tower in the evening
3
Watch Paris light up as the sun sets
4
Potentially catch the tower's sparkling lights afterward
And conveniently — your Seine cruise is already booked at 9:15 PM, so the Eiffel Tower sparkling from the water at night is already on the agenda! An evening summit earlier that day would flow perfectly into it.
