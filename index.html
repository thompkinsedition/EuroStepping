💡 Sunday Strategy Tips

Versailles on Sunday (Jul 5)

Busiest day BUT the only day for the Musical Fountains Show (Apr–Oct). Book the earliest 9am timed entry, do the Palace first, then enjoy the fountains in the gardens that afternoon.

Musée d'Orsay

Closed Mondays, so packed on Sundays. Best strategy: arrive 30 min before opening (~9am) or go very late afternoon (~4pm). Closes at 6pm on Sundays.

The Louvre

Timed entry is mandatory — do not show up without a booking. If early morning is sold out, try 1pm when crowds thin. Closed Tuesdays. Go on Wednesday Jul 8.
